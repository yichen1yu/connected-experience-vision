Ask Red Hat
CVE-2024-6345 is a high-severity vulnerability in OpenSSL 3.0.x affecting RHEL 8 and RHEL 9. The patched version is openssl-3.0.7-27.el9 for RHEL 9 and openssl-3.0.7-27.el8 for RHEL 8. Apply the erratum RHSA-2024:6345 to remediate. 📄 KB#7045231

Affected packages: openssl, openssl-libs, openssl-devel. Systems with custom kernel modules may require dependency resolution before patching. 📄 KB#7045298
